Featured Research of the Day

THE THESIS

Sim-to-Sim Transfer of Low-Dimensional Diffusion Policy for SO-ARM100 Pick-and-Place Using Webots Demonstrations

Introducing the Pipeline!Evaluated a low-dimensional Diffusion Policy for SO-ARM100 pick-and-place in Webots, focusing on its ability to generalize to novel simulated environments. Developed the experimental pipeline for teleoperated data collection, randomized dataset generation, model training, and evaluation using 300 demonstrations and a 14-dimensional state representation. Across 200 fully autonomous trials, the policy achieved 0% success due to grasp-initiation failures, while manual grip assistance enabled a 60.5% mean success rate, indicating that the learned policy could execute transport and placement but struggled with contact-rich grasping. Read the thesis or view the repository.
Build a pipeline without hardware!
Developed an accessible, reproducible Webots-based pipeline for diffusion-based robotic manipulation without requiring physical hardware.
Does the policy generalize?
Designed randomized environments to evaluate policy generalization across novel object/goal poses, workspace bounds, and robot states.
Still can't grasp it?
Identified grasp initiation as the primary failure point, revealing limitations of the 14D state representation for contact-rich manipulation.
