6dMetrix: Sales Intelligence

For small businesses that want sales reports without rebuilding spreadsheets by hand. It imports sales from CSV with a row-by-row validation preview, and one sale can hold several line items. It tracks stock automatically as sales come in, flags low and out-of-stock products, and generates dashboards plus downloadable PDF and CSV reports. Products and categories are fully user-defined, so it fits any type of goods, from motor parts to farm produce. The company name is configurable.

Python · TypeScript · React · Vite · FastAPI · PostgreSQL · SQLAlchemy · SQLite · Recharts · ReportLab

THESIS // 論文

Sim-to-Sim Transfer of Low-Dimensional Diffusion Policy for SO-ARM100 Pick-and-Place Using Webots Demonstrations
Python
Diffusion Policy is cool & amazing!For my undergrad thesis, I evaluated a low-dimensional Diffusion Policy for SO-ARM100 pick-and-place in Webots, focusing on its ability to generalize to novel simulated environments. I developed the experimental pipeline for teleoperated data collection, randomized dataset generation, model training, and evaluation using 300 demonstrations and a 14-dimensional state representation. Across 200 fully autonomous trials, the policy achieved 0% success due to grasp-initiation failures, while manual grip assistance enabled a 60.5% mean success rate, indicating that the learned policy could execute transport and placement but struggled with contact-rich grasping. Read the thesis or view the repository.
Build a pipeline without hardware!
Developed an accessible, reproducible Webots-based pipeline for diffusion-based robotic manipulation without requiring physical hardware.
Does the policy generalize?
Designed randomized environments to evaluate policy generalization across novel object/goal poses, workspace bounds, and robot states.
Still can't grasp it?
Identified grasp initiation as the primary failure point, revealing limitations of the 14D state representation for contact-rich manipulation.
