Sim-to-Sim Transfer of Low-Dimensional Diffusion Policy for SO-ARM100 Pick-and-Place Using Webots Demonstrations

    
Python

    

      
Evaluated a low-dimensional Diffusion Policy for SO-ARM100 pick-and-place in Webots, focusing on its ability to generalize to novel simulated environments. Developed the experimental pipeline for teleoperated data collection, randomized dataset generation, model training, and evaluation using 300 demonstrations and a 14-dimensional state representation. Across 200 fully autonomous trials, the policy achieved 0% success due to grasp-initiation failures, while manual grip assistance enabled a 60.5% mean success rate, indicating that the learned policy could execute transport and placement but struggled with contact-rich grasping.
